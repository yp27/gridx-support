For help with spaces, items, QR codes, NFC tags, or local data, send a short description and the diagnostic report available inside Grid.X.

Email Grid.X Support

Before contacting support

  1. Open My → About → Support.
  2. Choose Share diagnostics.
  3. Include a short description of what happened and, for camera, Photos, or NFC issues, your iPhone model and permission status.

The diagnostic report contains only the Grid.X version, build number, and iOS version. It does not include space names, item names, identifiers, notes, or photos.

Local data and recovery

Grid.X 1.0 stores its library locally. Support cannot retrieve a deleted library from a Grid.X server because this release has no Grid.X account or cloud-sync service. An Apple device backup may contain app data according to your iOS backup settings.

Please do not send personal inventory photos unless support explains why they are needed and you choose to share them.
