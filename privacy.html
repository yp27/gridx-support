Privacy Policy · Version 1.0

Your library stays on your iPhone.

Grid.X is a local-first inventory organizer. Version 1.0 requires no account and does not operate a cloud-sync service.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Data stored on your device

Spaces, zones, item names, notes, status history, and photos you choose are stored in the app’s local SwiftData library on this device. Grid.X does not independently upload this library.

iOS may include app data in an encrypted device or iCloud backup according to your device settings and Apple’s backup policies. Grid.X does not independently upload or control those system backups.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking

Version 1.0 contains no advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking, or third-party tracking SDK. Grid.X does not sell user data.

AI recognition is disabled in the App Store Release of version 1.0. Photos, item data, and space data are not sent to an AI service by this release.

Permissions

  • Camera: scans signed Grid.X QR labels and captures a photo only when you choose.
  • Photos: lets you choose a photo to attach to an item or space.
  • NFC: reads and writes signed Grid.X NDEF tags on a compatible physical iPhone.

These permissions are requested only when their associated feature is used. Manual entry and in-app QR display remain available without NFC.

QR and NFC label security

Grid.X signs QR and NFC payloads with an app-generated key stored in the device Keychain. The app rejects unsigned or modified Grid.X payloads. A label may still reveal that it is a Grid.X label; do not place labels where that fact is sensitive.

Deleting data

Uninstalling Grid.X deletes its local app library from the device, subject to your system backup state. If startup recovery is shown, you may also choose Reset local library; this requires confirmation and permanently deletes local spaces, zones, items, notes, and photos.

隐私政策摘要

Grid.X 1.0 无需账号，资料库保存在您的 iPhone 本地，不提供 Grid.X 云同步服务。此版本不包含广告、分析、跨 App 跟踪或第三方跟踪 SDK，也不会将照片、物品或空间资料发送至 AI 服务。

相机、相册和 NFC 权限仅在您主动扫描标签、选择或拍摄照片、读写 Grid.X 标签时使用。如有隐私问题，请联系 my.yang.peng@icloud.com。
